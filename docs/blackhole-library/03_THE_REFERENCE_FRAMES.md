## 📡 EVENT HORIZON TELESCOPE — the only NON-render reference

M87* (2019) and Sgr A* (2022) are **measurements**, not visualisations — VLBI interferometry
across an Earth-sized array. They are blurry because the array is the aperture, not because the
physics is soft.

**Evidence OF:** that the shadow size prediction is *real*. EHT confirmed a bright ring around a
dark centre at the size GR predicts from the mass. When our R1 test asks for **2.6× the
horizon**, that is not a stylistic target — it is a measured property of the universe.

⛔ **Do NOT use EHT as a LOOK reference.** Its resolution is instrument-limited; matching its
blur would be matching a telescope's limitations, not a black hole.
